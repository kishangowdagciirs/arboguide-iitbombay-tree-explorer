4. Field test procedure

  1. Move away from vehicles, metal fences, magnetic phone mounts and electrical equipment.
  2. Hold the phone upright in portrait orientation, with the top edge pointing forward.
  3. Stand still for 10 seconds and observe the stability indicator.
  4. Slowly rotate one full circle. The heading should change smoothly and return near its starting value.
  5. Repeat in a different open location and compare the readings.
Stability describes how much readings fluctuate over the recent few seconds. A stable reading can still have a constant directional offset; this page cannot certify magnetic calibration or absolute heading accuracy.
